# Smart India Hackathon Presentation & Jury Demo Guide
## Problem Statement: SIH26102 — AI-Based Detection of Fraud, Anomalies and Inefficiencies in MPLAD Implementation

---

### 1. Problem Statement Framing & Value Proposition
- **The Challenge**: The MPLAD scheme allocates ₹5 Crore annually per Member of Parliament across India for local infrastructure development. Monitoring thousands of distributed projects for cost overruns, timeline delays, repeat contractor inefficiencies, and duplicate billing is impossible through manual inspection alone.
- **The Solution**: An interpretable **AI-Assisted Anomaly & Investigation Prioritization Engine** that processes project data, highlights suspicious patterns, and empowers vigilance officers to prioritize high-risk audits.
- **Key Tone to Jury**: We emphasize that **AI assists, while human auditors decide**. We do not make automated legal accusations of fraud; we calculate an **Investigation Priority Score (0–100)** with Explainable AI factor breakdown.

---

### 2. Live Demo Script (Step-by-Step for Presentation)

#### Step 1: Single-Form Smart Authentication
- Open `login.html`. Point out to the jury:
  - There is **no role dropdown**.
  - Login as `investigator@demo.com` / `Investigator@123`.
  - The system automatically detects the Investigator role from MongoDB and customizes the navigation.
  - Explain: If a citizen logs in with any unknown email, the system automatically provisions a standard `USER` account with bcrypt security!

#### Step 2: Executive Analytics & Anomaly Screener
- Navigate to `dashboard.html`. Show the top KPI cards:
  - Total MPLAD works, Flagged Anomalies, Critical Priority, Cost Overrun volume.
  - Point to the interactive **Chart.js Risk Distribution Doughnut** and **State Anomaly Comparison Bar Chart**.
  - Review the **High Priority Watchlist**.

#### Step 3: 360° Project Dossier & Explainable AI (XAI)
- Click **"Analyze Dossier"** on a Critical Project (e.g. `MPLAD-2026-0007`).
- Highlight the **Explainable AI (XAI)** card:
  - Show the Priority Score meter (`87/100 - CRITICAL`).
  - Point out the **Factor Contribution Breakdown** (Cost 32%, Delay 24%, Contractor 18%, Payment 14%, Duplicate 12%).
  - Read the plain-language reasons generated by the engine.
  - Review the linked **Contractor Risk Profile**, **Milestone Timeline**, and **Payment Ledger**.

#### Step 4: Investigator Workflow & Blockchain Integrity Seal
- On the Project Dossier, click **"Start Formal Investigation"**.
- Go to `investigation-details.html`:
  - Log an inspection note.
  - Attach a site inspection image/document.
  - Click **"Verify On Blockchain"**: Watch the system compute the SHA-256 binary hash in real-time and verify it against the immutable smart contract anchor.

#### Step 5: GIS Geospatial Anomaly Map
- Open `map.html`:
  - Show the Leaflet.js interactive map centered on India.
  - Filter by "Critical Priority (Red)" and "Maharashtra / Uttar Pradesh".
  - Click any marker to reveal the popup card with direct dossier link.

#### Step 6: ML Model Governance & Lifecycle (Admin Demo)
- Logout and log in as `admin@demo.com` / `Admin@123`.
- Navigate to `ml-management.html`:
  - Show the active **Isolation Forest** model with silhouette, precision, and recall metrics.
  - Demonstrate retraining the model with configurable contamination rates.
  - Show the **Scoring Weights Calibration** tool to adjust Cost, Delay, and Contractor weights dynamically.
